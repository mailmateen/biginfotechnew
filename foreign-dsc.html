Description

What is Class 3 Individual Foreign User DSC ? Class 3 Digital Signature for Foreign Individual User Signing Certificate can be issued to any Individual subscriber who belongs to any country outside India, containing applicant name, eMail ID, PAN (Optional) in certificate. Any NRI, foreigners or foreign national user can buy or apply for this dsc. One can use this certificate mainly for ITR eFiling, ROC, MCA21, GST Registration, IEC Registration, EPFO or Provident Fund eFiling, Trademark and Patent eFiling, Form 16 Signing, Signing of GST Invoices or any other document signing in India. What is Class 3 Digital Signature Foreign Organization Signing & Encryption DSC ? As the name indicates, Organizations / Companies established outside India i.e Foreign Organizations can obtain and buy Class 3 Signing & Encryption (Combo) DSC for various platforms in India such as etendering, eprocurement, ebidding, eauction and many more. Foreign Organization User Digital Signature contains Individual Person as well as Organization name in digital certificate. What is Class 3 Digital Signature Foreign Organization Signing DSC ? As the name indicates, Organizations established outside India i.e Foreign Companies can obtain and buy Class 3 Signing DSC for various platforms in India such as etendering, eprocurement, ebidding, eauction and many more but Class 3 Signing Certificate works on very limited websites. To use it on all india etendering websites, buy Class 3 Signing & Encryption (Combo) DSC.
